Avish Mirjafari

Psychology · UC Riverside

I am Avish Mirjafari, a psychology undergraduate at the University of California, Riverside, with an interest in healthcare, psychology, and the ways people learn and communicate. As a pre-med student, I am interested in understanding people as individuals, including how their experiences and backgrounds shape the support they need. My coursework has encouraged me to think about health through biological, social, and cultural perspectives. I want to continue developing the communication and problem-solving skills that can help me contribute in people-focused settings.

Balancing school and full-time work has taught me to be intentional with my time and to adapt when plans change. I have learned to communicate about scheduling needs, organize competing responsibilities, and keep working toward long-term goals. I value being dependable, asking questions when I need clarification, and taking feedback seriously. Those habits matter to me because progress often comes from small improvements made consistently, whether I am completing a project, learning a new tool, or supporting someone in a conversation.

My experience as a Member Services Representative at Planet Fitness has been part of my professional development. Working in a member-facing environment has given me opportunities to practice communication and learn about workplace relationships. I am interested in the connection between a person's experience of an organization and the way its staff communicate. I want people to feel acknowledged and respected, and I continue to reflect on how listening and clear explanations can contribute to that experience.

I am also learning about ways to support student communication. One approach that has stood out to me is verbal prompting: using a helpful cue to encourage a student to express themselves. Learning from a coworker about this approach reminded me that effective communication sometimes begins with adjusting how we ask a question or offer support. It has strengthened my interest in psychology and in recognizing that different people may need different approaches to participate confidently.

Alongside my academic interests, I am building digital skills through projects involving HTML, CSS, game development, and online research. My projects have included a charity: water landing page, a water-themed game concept, and a CeraVe search-engine optimization research assignment. These experiences have helped me connect creative decisions with an audience's needs. I am learning to organize information, make instructions easier to follow, and revise work when something does not function as expected.

This portfolio brings together my education, work experience, and developing project interests. It reflects a learning process that is still in progress. As I move toward graduation and consider future opportunities, I hope to keep combining curiosity about human behavior with practical skills in communication and technology. I value work that gives me opportunities to learn from others, contribute thoughtfully, and make a useful difference in everyday interactions.
